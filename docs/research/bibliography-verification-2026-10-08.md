# Bibliography verification audit — 2026-10-08

This checks every publication reference in the 92-entry bibliography, with one independent DOI registry record or original-source link per entry. It is a bibliographic identity and metadata audit, not a full-text, claim-by-claim literature review.

## Verification methods and coverage

- 47 DOI-linked works compared with OpenAlex DOI metadata for title, first author, and year. Five date/indexing exceptions were checked against the version-of-record publisher or university archive.
- 45 original source URLs checked: 35 matching titles, 6 readable pages whose title or source needs context, 4 restricting automated access but corroborated in originating site search indexes or institutional records.
- For Justin Alexander, all 21 cited pages were directly retrieved and their first publication dates checked against the source pages.
- Links resolving and metadata identity checks alone do not guarantee all text is accurately paraphrased or all minor coauthor/venue fields are error-free.

## Corrections incorporated

- Dated the Failbetter Echo Bazaar post to March 3, 2010 (previously undated) and StoryNexus developer diary to August 5, 2012.
- Corrected the full headline of Alexander’s October 29, 2018 revelation-list article.
- Added missing AAAI proceedings pages for Chen et al. (2009), Mateas and Stern (2005), Nelson et al. (2006), and Rowe and Lester (2013).
- Added the ICIDS 2008 proceedings venue, LNCS 5334, and pp. 273–284 for Louchart et al. (2008).
- Reordered same-author references by year and title.
- Earlier fixes to the Bellaiche DOI and Salon article URL remain intact.

## Qualifications

- Four original URLs returned blocked or unavailable responses to direct automated access (GameSpot, UC eScholarship, and two MMORPG.com pages); each has a corroborating indexed record.
- Original-host editorial bylines can differ from named guest contributors (Rachel Thompson and Steve Williams).
- Same-author same-year in-text citations, particularly Justin Alexander and Emily Short, still require disambiguation when the final manuscript is produced.
- This audit did not read every full paper or verify how each claim uses its citation.
